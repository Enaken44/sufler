Privacy Policy

Updated November 3, 2026

In short: Sufler collects no data about you. No sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Your scripts

Scripts and settings are stored only on your device. If you choose an iCloud Drive folder, the app reads files from it — they stay in your iCloud. We have no access to them.

Voice

To follow your voice, speech is recognized by Apple's system. On recent iPhones, for most languages this happens entirely on the device. If a language isn't supported on-device, audio is processed by Apple's servers under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive or store audio.

Camera and microphone

Videos are saved only to your Photos app. We never see them or send them anywhere.

Remotes

Apple Watch, a second iPhone or iPad connect directly between your devices (local network or Bluetooth), without our servers.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
