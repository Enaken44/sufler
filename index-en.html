FAQ

The text doesn't follow my voice. Make sure Microphone and Speech Recognition are allowed in Settings → Sufler, and that Sufler's settings use the language you read in.

The remote can't find the teleprompter. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi or have Bluetooth on, and Local Network must be allowed in Settings → Sufler.

How much does it cost? Sufler is free: every feature is unlocked, with no ads and no sign-up.

Scripts from my Mac. Create a folder in iCloud Drive and choose it in Sufler’s settings — every .txt and .md file in it appears in your library.
